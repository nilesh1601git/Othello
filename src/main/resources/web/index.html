      How to play

      Black starts. Place a disc so that one or more straight lines of opposing discs are trapped between the new disc and another disc of your color. Every trapped disc flips.

      If you have no legal move, your turn passes. The game ends when neither player can move. The player with more discs wins.

      Show valid moves marks every square you can play. Turn it off to hide those marks. The squares stay playable either way.
